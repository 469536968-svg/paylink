PayLink

EIP-681 payment links & QR codes for stablecoins and native tokens — generated entirely in your browser.

No server. No signup. No tracking. Works offline once loaded.

Any wallet that supports EIP-681 will pre-fill the recipient, chain and amount. The raw address works everywhere else — paste it into any wallet's Send screen.

Why this exists. Most "payment link" tools phone home, require an account, or quietly take a cut. This one is a single static page: your address never leaves the tab.
